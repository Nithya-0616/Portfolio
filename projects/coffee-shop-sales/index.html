Six months of transactions from three coffee shops, analysed in Excel to find the morning rush, the best store and what customers really buy.

The question

A coffee chain had plenty of transaction data but no clear picture of performance. Which products sell, when do customers come in, and which of its three stores is doing best?

The data

Transaction-level sales data for January to June 2023: 149,116 transactions with date, time, store location, product category, product and price.

What I did

  • Reviewed the dataset structure, checked consistency and prepared fields for analysis.
  • Built Pivot Tables for monthly sales, category mix, top products, sales by hour and sales by store.
  • Designed an interactive Excel dashboard with KPI cards and slicers for month, store location and product category.

What the data showed

  • Coffee is the leading category by sales contribution.
  • Sales peak around 10 AM, making the morning the most important trading window of the day.
  • June was the strongest month, closing an upward trend through the first half of 2023.
  • Hell's Kitchen is the best-performing store of the three locations.

What I'd recommend

  • Match staffing and prep to the 10 AM peak.
  • Plan inventory around the top coffee products first.
  • Use the monthly trend for simple demand forecasting.
  • Compare store practices to bring the other two locations closer to Hell's Kitchen.
