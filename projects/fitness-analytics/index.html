The question

A gym wanted a clear view of its members and their training: demographics, popular workout types, calorie burn and habits like water intake and workout frequency.

The data

973 member records with 15 fields: age, gender, weight, height, BMI, body fat, heart rate (max, average and resting), session duration, calories burned, workout type, water intake, workout frequency and experience level.

What I did

  • Explored membership and fitness metrics and defined five headline KPIs.
  • Built views for members by workout type, water intake (box plots), gender split, age distribution and average calories by workout.
  • Added filters for gender, workout type and age group so the dashboard can be sliced by any segment.

What the data showed

  • Strength training has the most members, with Cardio close behind.
  • HIIT burns the most calories on average among the four workout types.
  • Members train about 3.3 days a week, and the average member is about 39 years old.
  • The split is fairly even: 511 male and 462 female members.

What I'd recommend

  • Expand Strength and Cardio capacity at peak times, since that is where most members are.
  • Promote HIIT classes to members whose goal is weight loss.
  • Use the age distribution to tailor programmes for the largest age groups.
