The question

A dairy business needed to understand which products, brands, regions and sales channels drive revenue, and how its sales were trending over time.

The data

4,325 records across 23 columns covering farm location and size, herd and land, product and brand, quantity and price, shelf life, production and expiry dates, stock levels, sales channel and reorder thresholds.

What I did

  • Explored the dataset and built Pivot Tables for product, brand, region, year, channel and farm size.
  • Designed six dashboard views with KPI cards and slicers for product and year.
  • Compared revenue across wholesale, retail and online channels and across small, medium and large farms.

What the data showed

  • Revenue declined from 2019 to 2022, the most important finding for the business.
  • Curd is the best-selling product, and Amul has the highest brand-level revenue.
  • Chandigarh records the highest regional revenue in the analysis.
  • Performance differs across sales channels, pointing to where distribution can improve.

What I'd recommend

  • Investigate the causes of the 2019–2022 decline before it continues.
  • Prioritise production and stock for the best-selling products.
  • Use the existing stock thresholds and reorder quantities to plan inventory.
  • Study regional and channel gaps to find expansion opportunities.
