The brief

Premier Club needed a central digital platform: a public website, online membership applications, a secure member portal with a professional directory, and an admin panel to run it all. The Business Requirements Document described the needs at a high level; the development and testing teams needed detailed, testable requirements.

The input

The supplied Business Requirements Document (BRD), covering business objectives, scope, stakeholders and high-level functional requirements for three connected components.

What I did

  • Studied the BRD and identified objectives, stakeholders, scope, assumptions, constraints, dependencies and out-of-scope items.
  • Wrote 36 user stories for visitors, applicants, members and administrators, each with priority, pre- and post-conditions, acceptance criteria and alternate flows such as invalid input, duplicate applications and failed submissions.
  • Defined 16 business rules and 13 non-functional requirements covering security, role-based access, performance, accessibility and responsive design.
  • Modelled the system with a use case diagram, a membership-application swimlane and a login process flowchart.
  • Built a Requirements Traceability Matrix linking every business requirement to its user stories, business rules and 53 acceptance test scenarios.

Key business rules

  • Only approved members can activate portal accounts, and every application goes through admin review.
  • Duplicate applications are caught using the applicant's email address.
  • Only published content is visible to the public, and admins see only the modules their role allows.
  • The member directory respects each member's privacy settings.

The outcome

A complete, traceable SRS that gives the design, development and testing teams one shared reference, with every requirement traced back to a business need and forward to a test scenario.
